Why use this rather than a chatbot?

Only the record. The AI that writes a briefing is given only the council minutes and votes found for your question, and may use nothing else. It is told to treat anything that isn't in those passages as unknown, even well-known facts. The passages are listed under the briefing, each linked to the council's own minutes.

Every point linked and checked. Each point links to its source. Citations are checked against the source text, quotations word for word, and sentences no source supports are flagged. A flagged sentence stays in the briefing, marked, so you can see what is supported and what isn't.

Up to date. Councils' minutes are read in every day, and recent minutes are read again in case they have changed. The home page gives the date of the last update.

It says when there is nothing. If the record holds nothing relevant, you see "No records found", not a guess. Where the search finds no minutes at all, no briefing is written. Where it finds minutes that don't answer the question, the AI is told to say so, and to say what they do cover.

Balanced by design. Evidence is chosen across councils run by different parties, not only by relevance. It is drawn in turn from councils under each party's control, and under no overall control, with at most two passages from any one council. The same rules apply to every party: see Fair to every party.

Records searched in full. Every council minute and named vote the site reads, from May 2018, all searchable and linked: full council and cabinet meetings, and the boards and scrutiny committees of England's strategic authorities. Not every council is covered yet: the list of councils shows which are, and why the rest aren't. A question searches the last four years unless it asks about earlier ones.

Open. The method and the code are published, every briefing is labelled as written by AI, the site is free, and it doesn't track you. The method is on the How it works page, the code is open source, and the privacy page says what is kept: no cookies, no advertising and no tracking.

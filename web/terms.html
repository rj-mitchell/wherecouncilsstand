What the service is, and is not

  • Briefings are written by AI from the councils' minutes cited with them. Automatic checks catch many errors but not all, and votes are read from the minutes automatically. Always check the sources before relying on a briefing.
  • A briefing reports what councils' minutes record. Minutes are officers' summaries, not word-for-word records. A briefing is not a statement of fact about the matters discussed, and it is not legal, financial or other professional advice.
  • Where Councils Stand is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any council, the Local Government Association, Civica or Northgate. The official record is each council's own minutes, which every source links to.
  • The service is free and provided "as is". It may change, be paused (for example, once the daily budget is spent, only the sources are shown) or stop.

Corrections and takedown requests

Tell us if something on the site is wrong or unfair, or if you're named and something should not be shown.

We reply within 5 working days, and decide within 20 working days. Takedown requests, and anything that may be unlawful or harmful, are dealt with sooner.

Corrections cover everything the site says, not only briefings: a councillor's party on the date, which side of a named vote they're shown on, a council's party in overall control (or a strategic authority's mayor), coverage labels, the links between councils and authorities, and how briefings group councils. Votes, parties and control are read automatically from councils' minutes, and briefings are written by an AI model, so mistakes are possible. Reports are read by a person. Takedown requests from people named in the minutes who aren't councillors are dealt with first.

If you're a councillor, or you speak for a council: If you're named on this site explains the three routes (something is wrong, you've been characterised unfairly, or adding a statement in your own words), how we check who you are, and how to appeal a decision.

This site can correct only what it shows. Mistakes in the minutes themselves can only be corrected by the council: contact its democratic services team.

Corrections and takedown requests

Tell us if something is wrong, or if you're named and something should not be shown.

Votes and parties are read automatically from councils' minutes, and briefings are written by an AI model, so mistakes are possible. Reports are read by a person. Takedown requests from people named in the minutes are dealt with first.

This site can correct only what it shows. Mistakes in the minutes themselves can only be corrected by the council: contact its democratic services team.

Dear Democratic Services team,

I'm writing from Where Councils Stand (https://wherecouncilsstand.com), an independent public website that helps people find out what their council has discussed, decided and voted on. It reads the minutes councils already publish of the meetings of full council and cabinet, from May 2018 onwards, and every point it makes links back to the council's own record. It is a project of Where They Stand, which is applying to register as a charity in England and Wales.

Your committee site, https://great-yarmouth.cmis.uk.com/great-yarmouth, is hosted for you by Civica, and its robots.txt file asks automated tools not to read it (as on every committee site Civica hosts that we have looked at). We have respected that and left Great Yarmouth Borough Council out, rather than work around it. We would like to include Great Yarmouth, so I'm writing to ask whether you would be willing to do one of the following:

1. ask Civica to let our crawler in, by adding these two lines at the top of the site's robots.txt (everyone else would still be kept out):

   User-agent: WhereCouncilsStand
   Allow: /

2. or reply to confirm you are content for us to read the public meeting pages and published minutes, and we will ask Civica ourselves.

How it would read your site:

- Only public pages: the committee and meeting pages, attendance, and the published minutes of full council and cabinet.
- At most one request a second, following any Crawl-delay you set. Every page is kept once fetched, so nothing is read twice.
- The first load is roughly 1,000 pages over about half an hour. After that, a daily check for new meetings, re-reading only meetings from the last four months, because minutes can be corrected when they are approved.
- It identifies itself as: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; WhereCouncilsStand/0.1; +https://github.com/rj-mitchell/wherecouncilsstand)
- Names of people who aren't councillors are removed before anything is shown.

If you would rather we didn't, we will keep Great Yarmouth out, and the site will say why it isn't included. I'm happy to answer any questions, or to change how it reads the site to suit you.

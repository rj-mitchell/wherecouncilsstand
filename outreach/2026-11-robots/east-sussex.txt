Subject: Permission to read East Sussex County Council's published minutes (Where Councils Stand)

Dear Democratic Services team,

I'm writing from Where Councils Stand (https://wherecouncilsstand.com), an independent public website that helps people find out what councils and strategic authorities have discussed, decided and voted on. It reads the minutes they already publish of the meetings of full council and cabinet, from May 2018 onwards, and every point it makes links back to the published record. It is a project of Where They Stand, which is applying to register as a charity in England and Wales.

Until now the site has read East Sussex County Council's published meetings through the web service Modern.gov provides (mgWebService.asmx on https://democracy.eastsussex.gov.uk). It should have checked your site's robots.txt first, as it does for other systems, and didn't: I'm sorry. Your robots.txt asks automated tools not to read the site, so we have stopped. The 137 meetings already read are kept, but no longer shown or used, and nothing more will be read unless you agree.

The same site publishes the meetings of the Sussex and Brighton Combined County Authority, so this letter asks for them too.

We would like to include East Sussex, so I'm writing to ask whether you would be willing to do one of the following:

1. let our crawler in, by adding these two lines at the top of the site's robots.txt (everyone else would still be kept out):

   User-agent: WhereCouncilsStand
   Allow: /mgWebService.asmx

2. or reply to confirm you are content for us to read the published meetings and minutes through the web service.

How it would read your site:

- Only what is public: Modern.gov's web service for the committees, meetings, attendance and minutes of full council and cabinet, and minutes published as documents.
- At most one request a second, following any Crawl-delay you set. Every response is kept once fetched, so nothing is read twice.
- After the first load, a daily check for new meetings, re-reading only meetings from the last four months, because minutes can be corrected when they are approved.
- It runs on our server, at [the server's IP address, as given in the letter sent], and identifies itself as: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; WhereCouncilsStand/0.1; +https://github.com/rj-mitchell/wherecouncilsstand)
- Names of people who aren't councillors are removed before anything is shown.

If you would rather we didn't, we will keep East Sussex out, and the site will say why it isn't included. I'm happy to answer any questions, or to change how it reads the site to suit you.

Kind regards,

Dear Democratic Services team,

I'm writing from Where Councils Stand (https://wherecouncilsstand.com), an independent public website that helps people find out what councils and strategic authorities have discussed, decided and voted on. It reads the minutes they already publish of the meetings of the London Assembly, from May 2018 onwards, and every point it makes links back to the published record. It is a project of Where They Stand, which is applying to register as a charity in England and Wales.

Your committee site, https://meetings.london.gov.uk, has a robots.txt file asking automated tools not to read it. We have respected that and left the Greater London Authority out, rather than work around it.

We would like to include the Greater London Authority, so I'm writing to ask whether you would be willing to do one of the following:

1. let our crawler in, by adding these two lines at the top of the site's robots.txt (everyone else would still be kept out):

   User-agent: WhereCouncilsStand
   Allow: /mgWebService.asmx

2. or reply to confirm you are content for us to read the published meetings and minutes through the web service.

How it would read your site:

- Only what is public: Modern.gov's web service for the committees, meetings, attendance and minutes of the London Assembly, and minutes published as documents.
- At most one request a second, following any Crawl-delay you set. Every response is kept once fetched, so nothing is read twice.
- After the first load, a daily check for new meetings, re-reading only meetings from the last four months, because minutes can be corrected when they are approved.
- It runs on our server, at [the server's IP address, as given in the letter sent], and identifies itself as: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; WhereCouncilsStand/0.1; +https://github.com/rj-mitchell/wherecouncilsstand)
- Names of people who aren't councillors are removed before anything is shown.

If you would rather we didn't, we will keep the Greater London Authority out, and the site will say why it isn't included. I'm happy to answer any questions, or to change how it reads the site to suit you.
